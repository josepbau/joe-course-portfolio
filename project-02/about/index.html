Military Service

In November of 1998 I enlisted into the United States Air Force and shipped off to Basic Training at Lackland A.F.B in San Antonio, TX. in February of 1999. Over a period of 4 1/2 years I served as a Telecoommunications Systems Journeyman and served until October of 2003. During my enlistment I was stationed at various bases throughout the world including Kunsan AB, South Korea, Ramstein AB, Germany, Bourgas AB, Bulgaria, Bagram AB, Afghanistan, and Jacobobad AB, Pakistan. It was an honor to serve my country thoughout a difficult time when our nation was faced with acts of terrrorism. I'll never forget the missions and deployments we carried out and that was a part of to answeer the call when our country needed it. I can honestly say that at the moment they were some of the toughest times in my life but when I look back and reminisce, I can't help but think these were also some of the best times of my life. It was a privelage and an honor to have served.
